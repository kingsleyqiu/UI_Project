# Presentation script

Each line can be typed or spoken. Pip's replies are shortened here; the ticket on the right updates after every accepted answer. Choose **Start over** in the header between scenarios.

## 1. Complete a pickup order

| Customer | Pip |
| --- | --- |
| *(Talk to Pip)* | Hi! I'm Pip, and I'll take your order. Would you like pickup or delivery? |
| Pickup. | Pickup. What size? Small is 10 inches, medium is 12, and large is 14. |
| Medium. | Medium. Thin or hand-tossed crust? |
| Thin crust. | Thin crust. Which toppings? We have pepperoni, mushrooms, bell peppers, onions, and extra cheese. |
| Pepperoni. | Pepperoni. How many pizzas like this would you like? |
| One. | One pizza. How would you like to pay? It's a demo, so nothing is charged. |
| Demo card. | Here's your order: one medium thin-crust pizza with pepperoni, for pickup, paid by demo card. Total $18.92. Should I place the order? |
| Place order. | Charging your demo card and sending your order to the kitchen… Your order is in! Order number 1234. It'll be ready for pickup around 6:20 PM at 123 King St W, Oshawa. |

**Faster version:** say "a medium thin crust pepperoni pizza for pickup" as the first answer. Pip fills four answers and asks only how many and how to pay.

## 2. Retry an unsupported topping

Start at the toppings question.

| Customer | Pip |
| --- | --- |
| Pepperoni and anchovies. | Anchovies aren't on our menu. Which toppings? *(offers "Just pepperoni" and "Cheese only")* |
| Just pepperoni. | Pepperoni. How many pizzas like this would you like? |

The ticket keeps service, size and crust while toppings wait for a valid answer.

## 3. Fix an incomplete delivery address

Choose delivery and answer up to the address question.

| Customer | Pip |
| --- | --- |
| Simcoe Street. | I need the building number for Simcoe Street. What's the delivery address? |
| 55 Simcoe St N. | Got 55 Simcoe St N. Which city is 55 Simcoe St N in? *(offers delivery cities)* |
| Toronto. | We don't deliver to Toronto yet. We deliver in Oshawa, Whitby, Ajax, Pickering, Courtice, and Bowmanville. Try another address, or switch to pickup. |
| Oshawa. | Delivering to 55 Simcoe St N, Oshawa. How would you like to pay? |

The street is kept the whole time, so the customer only supplies what was missing.

## 4. Change the size at review

Start from the review question in scenario 1.

| Customer | Pip |
| --- | --- |
| Change the size. | Sure, let's change the size. What size? |
| Large. | Large. Here's your order: one large thin-crust pizza with pepperoni… Should I place the order? |
| Place order. | Your order is in! |

Shortcuts that also work at review: "make it large", "add mushrooms too", "remove the onions", or **Edit** beside any ticket line.

## 5. Recover from a speech-recognition problem

With voice on, stay silent after tapping the mic. Pip shows "I didn't hear anything. Tap the mic and try again, or type your answer." and stops listening automatically. If the mic mishears, the words appear in the answer box with a 2-second countdown: start typing to stop it, correct the text, and press **Send**.

Other things to try anywhere: "go back", "repeat", "what can I say?", "pause", "start over".
