Set up your microphone

Choose Allow microphone, then approve Chrome’s permission prompt. This checks access and stops the test microphone stream immediately.

Voice starts only when you choose Start listening in the Domino’s assistant.

Chrome’s speech recognition may process audio using its recognition service. This extension sends food-related text to your locally configured Gemini backend. Personal, account and payment details belong in the website’s own fields.

After setup, return to your Domino’s Canada tab. Typing is available without microphone access.
